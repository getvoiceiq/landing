What can I paste or upload?

Comments copied from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google reviews, Trustpilot or WhatsApp. CSV, Excel and PDF files. Screenshots of comments. Or a YouTube link. Facebook and Instagram connection is coming.

Does it understand Kiswahili and Sheng?

Yes. It reads English, Kiswahili and Sheng, including sentences that mix them. It can still misread slang or sarcasm, which is why every finding shows the original comment.

How accurate is it?

It is AI, so it can make mistakes. We show the comments behind each finding so you can judge for yourself, and we are testing it on real Kenyan comments. Tell us when it gets something wrong and we will fix it.

Is my data safe?

Your comments are used only to produce your report and are never sold. You can delete them at any time. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I pay, and does it renew by itself?

Pay with M-Pesa or card. Monthly plans last 30 days and never renew by themselves. If a payment does not show, message us and we will check and activate your plan.

Can a politician or a church use it?

Yes. It reads public comments only. For political accounts, criticism is summarised without naming anyone, and the tone stays neutral.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Paste your comments, choose who you are, and press Start Analysis.
